Digital Library Management System

An integrated platform designed to automate cataloging, book issuance, return processing, and patron record administration with maximum efficiency.

Core Modules

  • Book Catalog: Add, edit, remove, and manage inventory counts.
  • Member Directory: Track student and faculty records with unique ID generation.
  • Issue & Returns: Instant book checkout with due-date alerts.
  • Reporting: Generate summaries of active loans and statistics.
